# Pricing and partner programs

## List prices
Prices on the website are list prices in US dollars. Implants list at $395 each and straight abutments at $150.

## Partner tiers
Practices in the partner program get 25% off list on implants, abutments and prosthetic parts. Course instructors and speakers get 35% off. Discounts don't apply to surgical kits, courses or shipping.

## Volume pricing
Orders of 20 or more implants in one order get an extra 5% off, on top of a partner discount.

## Quotes
A quote is valid for 30 days. Ask your sales rep for a quote for a large case or a new practice opening.
