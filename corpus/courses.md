# Education and courses

## Live courses
The two-day hands-on implant course costs $1,950 and includes 16 continuing-education credits. Courses are limited to 12 dentists so everyone places implants on models.

## Online courses
Online courses are free for partner practices and give 1 continuing-education credit per hour.

## Cancellations
Live course fees are refunded in full up to 30 days before the course. After that, the fee can move to a later course once.
