# Ordering

## Who can order
Licensed dentists and their staff can order once the practice account is verified. Verification needs the dentist's license number and state, and usually takes 1 business day.

## Placing an order
Orders are placed on the website or through a sales rep. Orders placed before 2 p.m. Pacific on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later ship the next business day.

## Order changes and cancellation
An order can be changed or cancelled until it is packed, which is usually within 2 hours of placing it. After that, refuse the delivery or use the exchange process.

## Backorders
If an item is out of stock, the rest of the order ships and the backordered item follows at no extra shipping cost. Sales reps can suggest an equivalent size that is in stock.
