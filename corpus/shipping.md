# Shipping

## Methods and costs
UPS Ground is $15 and takes 1 to 5 business days. Second-day air is $35. Next-day air is $55 and must be ordered by 2 p.m. Pacific. Orders over $1,000 ship by UPS Ground for free.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed as soon as the label is printed. Staff can also see it on the order in the order history.

## Where we ship
We ship within the United States only, including Alaska and Hawaii. Saturday delivery is not available.
