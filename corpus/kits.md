# Surgical kits

## Buying a kit
The full surgical kit costs $2,800 and includes the drills, drivers and a torque wrench for every implant size. Partner discounts don't apply to kits.

## Loaner kits
Partner practices can borrow a loaner kit for up to 14 days for their first cases. A $500 refundable deposit is charged and returned when the kit comes back complete.

## Drill replacement
Drills should be replaced after about 20 uses. Single replacement drills cost $65.
