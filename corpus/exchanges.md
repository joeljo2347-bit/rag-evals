# Exchanges

## Exchange windows
Unopened products can be exchanged for different sizes twice a year: during April and October for practices in California, and during March and September for practices in all other states. Exchange requests outside the window are declined.

## What can be exchanged
Only sealed, unexpired products in their original sterile packaging can be exchanged. Opened, expired or damaged items cannot. Products must have at least 12 months of shelf life left.

## How an exchange works
File the exchange form online or through your sales rep. Ship the items back with the prepaid label we email. Once the returned items are received and checked, the new items ship. If the new items cost more, the practice pays the difference; if they cost less, the difference is credited to the account.
