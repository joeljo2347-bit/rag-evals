# Billing and payment

## Payment methods
Practices can pay by card at checkout or, once approved for terms, by invoice. Invoices are due in 30 days.

## Late payments
Invoices unpaid after 45 days put the account on hold until they are paid. A hold blocks new orders but not exchanges already filed.

## Statements
A monthly statement is emailed on the first business day of each month and lists every invoice, payment and credit.
