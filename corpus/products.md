# Implants and compatibility

## Implant sizes
Implants come in diameters of 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 4.5 and 5.0 mm and lengths of 7 to 13 mm. The 3.0 mm implant is meant for narrow anterior sites such as lower incisors.

## Abutment compatibility
Abutments are matched by connection, not by implant diameter. The 3.0 mm implant uses the narrow connection; all other diameters share the regular connection. Using a regular abutment on a narrow implant is not supported.

## Cuff heights
Abutments come in cuff heights from 1 to 5 mm. Choose the cuff height from the soft-tissue thickness measured at the site.

## Healing caps
Each implant ships with a cover screw. Healing caps are sold separately, in 3 mm and 5 mm heights.
