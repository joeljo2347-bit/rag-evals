# Sterility, lots and recalls

## Packaging
Implants are sterilized by gamma irradiation and sealed in double packaging. Do not use an implant if either seal is broken or the expiry date has passed.

## Lot numbers
Every package carries a lot number and an expiry date. Record the lot number in the patient's chart; the peel-off label in the box is made for this.

## Recalls
If a lot is recalled, every practice that received it is called and emailed within 24 hours. Recalled products are replaced free of charge.
