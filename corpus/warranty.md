# Warranty

## Implant warranty
Implants carry a lifetime replacement warranty: if an implant fails to integrate, we replace it free of charge. The claim must include the lot number and the date of placement.

## Prosthetic parts
Abutments and prosthetic screws carry a 5-year warranty against fracture.

## What isn't covered
The warranty doesn't cover failures caused by placement outside the instructions for use, or the cost of the surgery or the restoration.
