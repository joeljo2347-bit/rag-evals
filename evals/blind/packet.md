# Help center (complete)

# Practice accounts

## Users
A practice account can have up to 10 staff users. The dentist who owns the account approves new users.

## Order history
Staff can see every order placed by the practice in the last 7 years, with tracking and invoices.

## Passwords and security
We never ask for a password by phone or email. Password reset links expire after 30 minutes.


# Billing and payment

## Payment methods
Practices can pay by card at checkout or, once approved for terms, by invoice. Invoices are due in 30 days.

## Late payments
Invoices unpaid after 45 days put the account on hold until they are paid. A hold blocks new orders but not exchanges already filed.

## Statements
A monthly statement is emailed on the first business day of each month and lists every invoice, payment and credit.


# Education and courses

## Live courses
The two-day hands-on implant course costs $1,950 and includes 16 continuing-education credits. Courses are limited to 12 dentists so everyone places implants on models.

## Online courses
Online courses are free for partner practices and give 1 continuing-education credit per hour.

## Cancellations
Live course fees are refunded in full up to 30 days before the course. After that, the fee can move to a later course once.


# Exchanges

## Exchange windows
Unopened products can be exchanged for different sizes twice a year: during April and October for practices in California, and during March and September for practices in all other states. Exchange requests outside the window are declined.

## What can be exchanged
Only sealed, unexpired products in their original sterile packaging can be exchanged. Opened, expired or damaged items cannot. Products must have at least 12 months of shelf life left.

## How an exchange works
File the exchange form online or through your sales rep. Ship the items back with the prepaid label we email. Once the returned items are received and checked, the new items ship. If the new items cost more, the practice pays the difference; if they cost less, the difference is credited to the account.


# Surgical kits

## Buying a kit
The full surgical kit costs $2,800 and includes the drills, drivers and a torque wrench for every implant size. Partner discounts don't apply to kits.

## Loaner kits
Partner practices can borrow a loaner kit for up to 14 days for their first cases. A $500 refundable deposit is charged and returned when the kit comes back complete.

## Drill replacement
Drills should be replaced after about 20 uses. Single replacement drills cost $65.


# Ordering

## Who can order
Licensed dentists and their staff can order once the practice account is verified. Verification needs the dentist's license number and state, and usually takes 1 business day.

## Placing an order
Orders are placed on the website or through a sales rep. Orders placed before 2 p.m. Pacific on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later ship the next business day.

## Order changes and cancellation
An order can be changed or cancelled until it is packed, which is usually within 2 hours of placing it. After that, refuse the delivery or use the exchange process.

## Backorders
If an item is out of stock, the rest of the order ships and the backordered item follows at no extra shipping cost. Sales reps can suggest an equivalent size that is in stock.


# Pricing and partner programs

## List prices
Prices on the website are list prices in US dollars. Implants list at $395 each and straight abutments at $150.

## Partner tiers
Practices in the partner program get 25% off list on implants, abutments and prosthetic parts. Course instructors and speakers get 35% off. Discounts don't apply to surgical kits, courses or shipping.

## Volume pricing
Orders of 20 or more implants in one order get an extra 5% off, on top of a partner discount.

## Quotes
A quote is valid for 30 days. Ask your sales rep for a quote for a large case or a new practice opening.


# Implants and compatibility

## Implant sizes
Implants come in diameters of 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 4.5 and 5.0 mm and lengths of 7 to 13 mm. The 3.0 mm implant is meant for narrow anterior sites such as lower incisors.

## Abutment compatibility
Abutments are matched by connection, not by implant diameter. The 3.0 mm implant uses the narrow connection; all other diameters share the regular connection. Using a regular abutment on a narrow implant is not supported.

## Cuff heights
Abutments come in cuff heights from 1 to 5 mm. Choose the cuff height from the soft-tissue thickness measured at the site.

## Healing caps
Each implant ships with a cover screw. Healing caps are sold separately, in 3 mm and 5 mm heights.


# Returns

## Returns for credit
Sealed products can be returned for account credit within 30 days of delivery. A 15% restocking fee applies. Refunds to the original card are not offered; returns are always credited to the practice account.

## Wrong or damaged items
If we shipped the wrong item or it arrived damaged, report it within 7 days. We replace it at no charge and send a prepaid return label. No restocking fee applies.


# Shipping

## Methods and costs
UPS Ground is $15 and takes 1 to 5 business days. Second-day air is $35. Next-day air is $55 and must be ordered by 2 p.m. Pacific. Orders over $1,000 ship by UPS Ground for free.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed as soon as the label is printed. Staff can also see it on the order in the order history.

## Where we ship
We ship within the United States only, including Alaska and Hawaii. Saturday delivery is not available.


# Sterility, lots and recalls

## Packaging
Implants are sterilized by gamma irradiation and sealed in double packaging. Do not use an implant if either seal is broken or the expiry date has passed.

## Lot numbers
Every package carries a lot number and an expiry date. Record the lot number in the patient's chart; the peel-off label in the box is made for this.

## Recalls
If a lot is recalled, every practice that received it is called and emailed within 24 hours. Recalled products are replaced free of charge.


# Warranty

## Implant warranty
Implants carry a lifetime replacement warranty: if an implant fails to integrate, we replace it free of charge. The claim must include the lot number and the date of placement.

## Prosthetic parts
Abutments and prosthetic screws carry a 5-year warranty against fracture.

## What isn't covered
The warranty doesn't cover failures caused by placement outside the instructions for use, or the cost of the surgery or the restoration.
